SLEEK SUDOKU

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

In short: Sleek Sudoku does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos. Your games and settings stay on your phone. The app sends crash reports, optional anonymous usage statistics, and the information Google needs to show ads.

This policy explains what data the Sleek Sudoku app (“the app”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by MATLEVIC Labs (“MATLEVIC”, “we”, “us”). If you have any questions, contact nicoleangwenyi43@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your phone

These are kept in the app’s own storage on your device and are never sent to us:

  • Your current game, game history, statistics, streaks and achievements
  • Your settings, such as theme, sound, haptics and the daily reminder time
  • How many auto notes uses you have left today
  • A random ID created when you first open the app. It is not linked to your identity

The daily reminder is scheduled on your phone. No server is involved, and turning it off cancels it. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner while you play and on the results screen, a full-screen ad between some games (never during a puzzle), and an optional video ad you can choose to watch for an extra hint or auto notes use.

To show and measure ads, Google collects your device’s advertising ID and device information such as model, operating system and IP address. Ads are personalised only where you allow it. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you are asked for consent when you first open the app, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings, Privacy policy, Ad privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads). Learn how Google uses this data in its explanation for apps that use Google services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics (PostHog), optional

To understand how the game is played and improve it, the app sends anonymous events to PostHog, hosted in the European Union. Examples: a game was started or finished, its difficulty and time, a hint was used, a setting was changed. Each event carries the random ID, your device model, operating system, app version and language. No name, email or game content beyond these events is sent, and screen recording is turned off.

This is on by default. You can turn it off at any time with “Share anonymous usage data” under Settings, Privacy policy. See PostHog’s privacy policy.

Crash reports (Sentry)

When the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry so the problem can be fixed. It contains the technical error, device model, operating system, app version, the random ID and a short list of recent app events (such as “game started”). It contains no personal details. Crash reports are always sent. See Sentry’s privacy policy.

Why we process this data

  • Ads: to keep the game free. Personalised ads rely on your consent where the law requires it.
  • Usage statistics: our legitimate interest in improving the game. You can opt out at any time.
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google, PostHog and Sentry) for the purposes described. All data is sent over encrypted connections.

Retention

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app. Usage statistics and crash reports are kept by PostHog and Sentry only as long as needed to analyse them, and in any case no longer than their standard retention periods. Google retains advertising data under its own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off usage statistics in the app at any time.
  • Change your ad consent under Ad privacy choices (EEA and UK), or reset your advertising ID in your phone settings.
  • Ask us to access or delete the usage and crash data linked to your random ID by emailing nicoleangwenyi43@gmail.com. Because this data is not linked to your name, we may ask for details such as your device model and when you used the app so we can find it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object, to restrict processing, or to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Sleek Sudoku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you want their data removed, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
